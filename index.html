Technical detail See the 4-stage enforcement ladder Meta runs on restricted domains
  1. Level 00

    Domain categorized

    Meta's crawler scans your destination URL and tags your domain as restricted. You receive no notification.

  2. Level 01

    Event metadata blocked

    Your pixel still fires, but Meta strips product names, values, and categories from incoming events before ingestion.

  3. Level 02

    Purchase events blocked

    The conversion signal Meta's algorithm optimizes on stops flowing. Delivery quality degrades across all campaigns.

  4. Level 03

    All events blocked

    Pixel data is dropped entirely. Your ad account has no signal to optimize on, and creative-level learning resets.

How it works

Four components. One system.

  1. 01

    Compliant landing page

    Your ads point to a Meta-approved landing page on a clean domain. We help you set it up or you bring your own.

  2. 02

    Session stitching

    When a visitor clicks through to checkout, we pass session identity to your main store. Attribution stays intact. Nothing changes for the customer.

  3. 03

    Event cleansing

    Pixel and server events from both domains flow through our proxy. Restricted keywords are stripped from payloads. PII is hashed. The event source URL is rewritten to the compliant domain.

  4. 04

    Conversions API delivery

    Clean events are forwarded to Meta via CAPI using your pixel access token. Optimization resumes with compliant signal.

FAQ

Questions we get often.

If yours isn't here, email us at hello@savemypixel.com. We usually reply same-day.

01How is this different from just using Meta's Conversions API directly?

CAPI alone doesn't solve domain categorization. If Meta has flagged your domain, sending events from it through CAPI still gets them blocked. We solve it by routing events from a separate compliant domain, and by cleansing payloads before they reach Meta. CAPI is the delivery mechanism. What we do is the compliance layer around it.

02Do I have to rebuild my site?

No. Your main store stays exactly as it is. We add one compliant landing page on a new domain. Ads point to the landing page, customers click through to your main store for checkout. Your Shopify theme, your product pages, your funnels — all untouched.

03How long until I see results?

For brands with a healthy ad account, event flow is restored within 48 hours of go-live. For flagged domains, Meta's optimization algorithm typically needs 7–14 days of clean signal to fully re-learn. Ad approval patterns tend to improve within the first week.

04What platforms do you support?

Shopify via our native app (fastest install, usually under an hour). Any other platform (WooCommerce, BigCommerce, custom builds) via our script tag and managed setup. If you're on Wix or Squarespace, talk to us first — we'll tell you honestly if it'll work.

05My domain is already flagged. Can you still help?

Yes. Most of our customers come to us after enforcement has already started. We don't un-flag your existing domain — that rarely works. What we do is route all future ad traffic through a new clean domain, which is what restores your pixel signal and optimization.

06Do I need a new Meta pixel?

Usually not. We route clean events through your existing pixel via its access token. You keep your historical data, learning, and audience signal. In rare cases where a pixel is deeply compromised, we'll recommend a new one and help you migrate.

07Do you manage ads or make creative?

No. We're data and compliance infrastructure. Your team or agency handles buying and creative. We work alongside most major agencies and can refer you to ones who specialize in restricted categories if you need one.

08How are you different from Popsixle?

Popsixle pioneered this space and built a great product. Our differences are in pricing and simplicity: we charge a flat monthly rate instead of base-plus-usage, and we handle unlimited revenue at the Scale tier. For most brands above $100K/mo in tracked revenue, we cost meaningfully less. For brands below $25K/mo, Popsixle is cheaper. Both tools work.

09What about PII and privacy?

All user data is hashed with SHA-256 before it reaches Meta. We never store raw PII longer than 24 hours. Events are sent via Conversions API using your pixel access token, not a user login. We support GDPR and CCPA deletion requests. SOC 2 certification is in progress.

10How does the revenue bracket work?

We look at the gross revenue from Purchase events we process for you each month. If you stay inside your bracket, your bill doesn't change. If you consistently exceed it (two months in a row), we'll reach out and recommend upgrading. Nobody gets a surprise bill or a throttled pixel.

11Is there a free trial?

We don't run a time-limited free trial. Instead, every prospect gets a free 20-minute compliance review with our team before signing up — we audit your domain, pixel, and Meta account health, and tell you honestly whether we can help. If you do sign up, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first month.

12Is this a policy workaround?

No. We remove restricted terms from event payloads and route events from a compliant domain. That's policy alignment and privacy-aware data delivery, both explicitly supported by Meta's own Conversions API specification. We don't spoof conversions, fake identities, or send disallowed data.
